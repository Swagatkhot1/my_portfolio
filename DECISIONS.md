# Portfolio personalization decisions

## Summary
- Replaced the template resume data with a Zod-validated, typed site-content module populated only from the supplied resume.
- Kept the Astro/React/Tailwind layout and existing visual components, while removing template work, projects, photos, hackathons, and blog content.
- Wired portfolio sections and metadata to the centralized content module; sections with no supplied content are omitted.
- Input still needed for a personal site URL, project repositories/demos, research advisor, education start date/coursework, award dates, and any desired project stacks beyond the technologies stated in the resume. The award project descriptions and certifications could not be represented by the requested schema.

## Recon findings
- This is an Astro 6 portfolio with React islands and Tailwind CSS. The template’s established portfolio convention is a single typed data module (`src/data/resume.tsx`) consumed by page and section components; its separate Astro content collection was used only for the template blog.
- The main route is `src/pages/index.astro`, shared metadata lives in `src/layouts/Layout.astro`, and `src/components/HomePage.tsx` composes the portfolio sections.
- Template placeholders were concentrated in `src/data/resume.tsx`, the blog MDX files, and the blog UI/routes.

## Assumptions and omissions
- The provided LinkedIn address was normalized to `https://linkedin.com/in/swagat-khot`; the resume supplies no GitHub, project, demo, school, or employer URLs, so those fields are omitted.
- “Summer Studio Program (AdviseAssist)” is treated as the organization and “AdviseAssist” as the role because the resume’s formatting does not explicitly separate organization and title.
- The Department of ISE research entry is placed in `research`, not general experience, because its role is explicitly Undergraduate Research Assistant.
- The resume does not identify the research advisor, coursework, project stacks, project links, or award dates; those optional fields are omitted.
- The objective was adapted as the site bio and tagline without adding claims. Certifications have no field in the requested schema and could not be placed cleanly.
- The resume gives an expected graduation date but no start date; education start is omitted.
- The fall pilot wording is retained as a current status; no pilot outcome is asserted.
- The Summer Studio, Department of ISE research, and UT Knoxville education entries use the University of Tennessee mark supplied by the site owner.
- The Summer Studio organization name and logo link to the official 2026 cohort announcement in a new tab.
- West Tennessee Consulting uses the logo supplied by the site owner; its name and logo link to `https://www.westtn.consulting/en`.
- Added a sticky top navigation bar, a Certifications section (CS50 (Python), TestOut PC Pro, TestOut Network from the resume), timeline-style experience/research entries, and optional image galleries on experience, research, and project entries.
- The site owner supplied their GitHub profile (`https://github.com/WTCSwagat`); it is now in `basics.links` and shows in the dock navbar with the GitHub icon. AdviseAssist screenshots are still pending; the `images` fields are ready for them.
- Projects accept an optional `video` (`src` plus `poster`). When present it replaces the still as the card cover and plays as a silent autoplaying loop, so every supplied image stays visible in the card's gallery instead of being consumed by the cover.
- EF 152 was promoted to `featured` so its demo clip gets the wide card's tall media panel, which suits portrait phone footage. Its cover video is center-cropped from the top so the clock face survives the short, wide crop used on phones.
- The supplied clip (`IMG_4556.MOV`, 1080p HEVC, 28s, 31 MB) was re-encoded to 720p H.264 at CRF 28 with audio stripped, taking it to 1.9 MB; H.264 in MP4 is the widest-supported combination and muting is what allows browsers to autoplay it. A poster frame is extracted so something renders before the video loads.
- The clock face photo was cropped to drop the storage bin below the workbench and re-saved as a progressive JPEG.
- The mono micro-labels (section numbers, dates, tags, nav rail) were written as literal px, so `CONFIG.typography.baseFontSize` never reached them and they stayed at 9-11px. They now use a rem-based `text-label-sm/label/label-lg` scale defined in `global.css`, which both enlarges them and makes the base-size setting actually govern the whole page. Secondary body copy was aligned to the `0.95rem` already used by bullets.
- The sidebar index is vertically centred between the header and the social row, matching the supplied reference. It uses `safe` centring because that rail scrolls: on a short viewport, plain centring would push the first sections above the scroll origin where they cannot be reached.
- Class standing is a separate optional `education[].standing` field rather than being folded into the degree string, so it can be updated on its own each autumn. It is currently "Sophomore"; At-a-glance and education still carry the factual standing, while About prose and the sidebar tagline focus on voice rather than class year.
- About was rewritten from the site owner's draft into three short paragraphs (identity + POV, current work, interests). Spelling cleaned up (technology, basketball); name meaning set as "I'm Swagat — the name means welcome" instead of a parenthetical; "user's" kept as "a user's"; dangling "also" dropped; sports kept as a short list rather than "all the sports." Tagline shortened to match that voice without inventing employers or metrics.
- Deleted `src/components/project-card.tsx`, an unused leftover of the template's card; `HomePage.tsx` has its own.
- The two EF 151 launcher photos were supplied as screenshots with a decorative orange frame baked into the edges, so the frame was measured in the pixels and cropped away before anything else; the head-on view then lost a strip of workshop background above the backboard and a little dead wall on the left, and the top-down view lost a thin light gutter on its left edge. EF 151 stays non-featured: both photos are panoramic, and the featured layout's tall media panel would slice a landscape shot into a vertical sliver, the same problem EF 152 had, whereas the non-featured card's short wide cover strip fits them.
- The type was reset without touching the layout: Instrument Sans → **Geist** (body/UI), Instrument Serif → **Newsreader** (display), Geist Mono → **JetBrains Mono** (micro-labels). Newsreader is a text serif rather than a display one, so the lede and headings hold up at the sizes this layout sets them; the old faces' packages were removed. Only two knobs moved with it — `.font-serif` now sets `font-weight: 450` (Newsreader at 400 reads thin next to Geist) and its optical letter-spacing eased from `-0.012em` to `-0.006em`, since Newsreader is already wider and looser than what it replaced. Mono swapped safely because both faces use the same 0.6em advance, so the tracked labels keep their measured widths. Reverting is a `pnpm add` of the three old packages plus those five lines in `global.css`.
- Copy was rewritten for plain readability without adding a claim the resume does not make: résumé shorthand ("categorizes emails, surfaces policy context, and pre-drafts replies") became sentences that say who the thing is for and what it does; the AdviseAssist entry's second bullet was split in two because it was carrying Graph/OAuth, retrieval, and PII scrubbing at once; awards moved the placement out of the title (`Coding & Programming — 1st place` / `FBLA, state level`); and the education kicker stopped saying "Coursework" when no coursework is listed ("In progress"). The About lede keeps the site owner's "the name means welcome" opening and its `welcome` emphasis.
- `Collapsible` measured `contentRef.scrollHeight`, but its only child carries `mt-8 xl:mt-10` and no border or padding sits between them, so that top margin collapsed *through* the wrapper and landed outside the box being measured. Every section panel therefore opened 37-46px short and clipped its own last rows — visible as the interests list losing "Table tennis" and "Lifting". The wrapper is now `flow-root` (a block formatting context stops the margin escaping) and the measurement rounds `getBoundingClientRect().height` up, since fractional content heights lost their last sub-pixel row to the integer `scrollHeight`.
- The basketball project lost its `featured: true`. Featured cards devote a tall panel to media, and that project has no image, so it was spending the panel on the fallback monogram. Un-featuring it also pairs it with EF 151 in the two-column grid; previously EF 151 was the only unfeatured project and sat alone in a two-column row with dead space beside it. Featured status now tracks whether a project actually has media worth showing: Vol Wash and EF 152 have it, the other two do not.
